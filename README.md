## 🎁 How to get a beat for free

1. Pick a beat from the **catalog** below.
2. Click its name to open the folder, then click `preview_30s.mp3` and press ▶ to listen (or watch it on YouTube).
3. Like it? Email **kuoorexcontact@gmail.com** with the beat name and we'll send you the full free MP3.
4. Use it in your song. Just credit `"Your Song Title (prod. Kuoorex)"` when you release it.

That's it — no account, no download button to hunt for, no fine print you need a lawyer for. Full rules are in [LICENSE_TIERS.md](LICENSE_TIERS.md) if you want the details, but the short version is: **free to use, credit us, we keep a small cut if your song makes money.**

Want the beat without a tag, or full ownership? Paid options (lease / exclusive) are below.
